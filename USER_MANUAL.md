# Smart DMS User Manual

This guide is for end users of the current Smart DMS application.

## 1. Sign in
1. Open the application in your browser.
2. Enter your email address or employee ID and password.
3. Click Sign In.
4. If you forget your password, use the Forgot Password link.

## 2. Use the dashboard
After signing in, the dashboard shows your available documents.
Use it to:
- search for documents
- filter by plant, department, customer, or status
- view approval status
- bookmark important records
- open recently viewed documents

## 3. Upload a document
1. Open the Upload page.
2. Choose the plant and department.
3. Enter the document number and revision number.
4. Select the correct category and library folder path.
5. Choose the file(s) to upload.
6. Submit the form.

Uploaded documents are saved and sent to approval.

## 4. Review approvals
If you are an approver, go to the Approvals page to review pending items.
You can:
- see the document metadata
- preview the document
- approve or reject it
- add comments when rejecting

## 5. Work with the document library
The document library helps users browse reference documents by category and folder.
Use it to quickly find procedures, manuals, and other stored documents.

## 6. Browse procedures and records
The Procedures section organizes procedure documents by category.
The Plant Assets and Customer Records pages help users browse information grouped by plant/department or customer.

## 7. Track updates and notifications
Use the tracking page to follow the progress of submissions.
Notifications help you stay informed about approvals and system updates.

## 8. Best practices
- Use clear document numbers and revision numbers.
- Choose the correct category and folder path before upload.
- Check plant, department, and customer values carefully.
- Contact the administrator for access issues or approval questions.

## Support
For sign-in issues, upload problems, or approval questions, contact the DMS administrator or your team lead.
